# Code of Conduct

Participation requires respectful, inclusive and evidence-based engagement.
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing and retaliation are not
acceptable. Criticism of code, methodology and accessibility findings is welcome
when directed at ideas and backed with evidence.

Until a dedicated community process exists, report conduct concerns privately
to the repository maintainers using GitHub's private maintainer contact where
available. Do not put sensitive accounts in public issues. The interim steward
may warn, moderate, or restrict participation, recording a public summary when
safe. Maintainers must recuse themselves from reports about their own conduct.

This starter policy must be supplemented with an independently accessible
reporting route before the project invites a larger public community.
